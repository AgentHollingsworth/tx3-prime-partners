The fine print, in plain language

How we keep this fair

Rates this high only work when the volume behind them is real. These rules protect the partners producing genuine business.

  • Commission and bonuses are paid on real client trading activity. Volume from accounts you own or control does not qualify.
  • Round-trip trades opened and closed with no genuine market exposure are excluded from bonus-qualifying volume.
  • Volume tiers must be spread across a reasonable number of active clients, not concentrated in a small handful of accounts.
  • Net deposits count toward milestones once funds have remained in-account for 30 days.
  • Deposit milestones are based on net deposits — deposits minus withdrawals — not gross inflows.
  • Volume tiers re-qualify each calendar month based on that month's activity.
  • TX3 Markets may review partner activity and recover bonuses paid on volume or deposits later found to be non-genuine.
  • Rates and bonus schedules are subject to the full partner terms and may change with notice.
