The fine print, in plain language

How we keep this fair

Rates this high only work when the volume behind them is real. These rules protect the partners producing genuine business.

  • Commission and bonuses are paid on real client trading activity. Volume from accounts you own or control does not qualify.
  • Round-trip trades opened and closed with no genuine market exposure are excluded from bonus-qualifying volume.
  • Volume tiers must be spread across a reasonable number of active clients, not concentrated in a small handful of accounts.
  • Net deposits count toward milestones once funds have remained in-account for 30 days.
  • Deposit milestones are based on net deposits — deposits minus withdrawals — not gross inflows.
  • Volume tiers re-qualify each calendar month based on that month's activity.
  • TX3 Markets may review partner activity and recover bonuses paid on volume or deposits later found to be non-genuine.
  • Rates and bonus schedules are subject to the full partner terms and may change with notice.

The fine print, in plain language

How we keep this fair

Upfront payments only work when the clients behind them are real. These rules protect the partners producing genuine business.

  • One CPA per client, ever. A client who closes and reopens an account does not re-qualify.
  • The full CPA is recovered if the client withdraws their qualifying deposit within 90 days.
  • Your election of TX3 Prime or TX3 Direct is made at signup and applies to every client referred under that agreement. You may switch once per quarter with 30 days' notice; switches are never applied retroactively.
  • Only closed trades placed in the referred client's tracked account within the 30-day window count toward the lot minimum. Round-trip trades with no real market exposure do not count, and PAMM-generated volume does not qualify.
  • Clients must be unique and KYC-verified. Self-referrals and referrals of immediate family qualify only at a minimum $3,000 deposit and 25 lots, and are subject to review.
  • Monthly qualification caps apply at launch: 25 clients in the $100–$249 band, and 10 clients across the top two bands, per partner per month.
  • TX3 Markets may review partner activity and recover payments made on clients later found to be non-genuine.
